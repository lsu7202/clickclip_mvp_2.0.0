# Write a Script According to the Guidelines

Write a video narration script based on the provided Topic. 

# Objective:

The reader clearly understands the conclusion of the script. The conclusion presents clear actions. Clear actions are highly stimulating, such as buying or selling stocks or presenting investment judgments.

# Pipeline

1. Understand the topic.

2. Derive the claim of the topic. * This claim helps you realize how money works and teaches you how to make money.

3. Derive the insights and answers to convey to the reader.

4. Derive the relationship between the key evidence for the topic and the claim.

5. Write the text according to the provided Guideline.

# Output Format

- Absolutely do not use headings such as [Intro], [Body], or [Conclusion], or separators like '## Subtitle' or '---'.

- Output the text in the format of a 'video script,' speaking continuously from beginning to end as if a single speaker were speaking without interruption.

- Do not use any Markdown formatting, such as bold (**), asterisks (*), hyphens (-), or hashtags (#).

- Write using only text and punctuation (., ?, !).

# Guideline 


[1] Key Points:

- Use the structure of 'Claim - Explanation of Cause - Summary of Claim' to summarize and emphasize the claim once again at the end.

- Before explaining the cause, I declare that I will definitely explain the cause.

- Convey what you intend to say by using self-questioning and self-answering.

- Use colloquial and written language appropriately

- Frequently use sentences that conclude or summarize the preceding explanation to help the reader understand.

[2] Structure & Total Lenght:
* Total Length: Approximately 3,000 characters
* A form in which spoken words are transcribed into words without special characters.

# Educational Requirement (Common)

- Every script must contain genuinely educational content: the viewer must finish the video knowing something concrete they did not know before (facts, background knowledge, principles, or a practical lesson).
- Weave the educational content through the body naturally — teach while telling, never as a dry lecture block.
- At least one "did-you-know" level concrete fact or principle must appear in the body.
